Sip & Snack Buddy 0.6.0: history

- New History screen: tap "See history" on Today. Pick 7 or 30 days.
- Water: average a day, goal-hit days, and whether it's up or down on the days before ("Up 12% on the 7 days before").
- Steps: the same, once a few days of steps are saved. Steps are saved day by day from this version on.
- Medicine: "Taken 6 of 7 days", counted from the first day it was ticked off.
- Day by day list: water, steps, meals and medicine for each day.
- On Walking Selfie Stick's phone: "See her history" on the Her day tab shows her shared days the same way (what she shares, nothing more), and "See history" on My day shows his own.

Tap Update in the app, or install `SipSnack-0.6.0.apk` below. It keeps all logs.
