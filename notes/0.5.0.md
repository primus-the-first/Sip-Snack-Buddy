Sip & Snack Buddy 0.5.0: medication

- A Medication card on her Today screen, starting with Becoatin once a day at 8:00 am. One tap on Taken, then "Let's have it!" and a streak ("3 days in a row").
- A reminder at her time and one gentle follow-up two hours later if it isn't ticked off. Nothing more that day.
- Settings > Medications: change the time, add or remove medicines. The app only reminds and records; it never suggests doses.
- Her phone asks once whether to share medicine check-offs with Walking Selfie Stick. If yes, his "Her day" tab shows "Becoatin · Taken 8:12 am" or "Not yet". She can change it in Sharing.

Tap Update in the app, or install `SipSnack-0.5.0.apk` below. It keeps her logs.
