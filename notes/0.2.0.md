Sip & Snack Buddy 0.2.0: live sharing

- First open asks who's using the phone: Mini Boss Battle or Walking Selfie Stick.
- Her phone asks once: "Let Walking Selfie Stick cheer you on?" One tap and sharing runs by itself.
- His phone shows her day live: ring, last sip, meal times (if she allows) and her week. He can send cheers, and gets a notification when she hits her goal.
- She can choose what he sees, pause, or stop (which deletes the shared copy) from the "Sharing with Walking Selfie Stick" label on her Today screen.

Download `SipSnack-0.2.0.apk` below on each phone and tap it to install. It updates 0.1.x and keeps her logs.
