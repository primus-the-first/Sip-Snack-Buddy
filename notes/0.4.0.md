Sip & Snack Buddy 0.4.0: steps

- Steps, counted by the phone's own step sensor (no other app, works offline). A steps bar sits under the water ring: "3,240 of 5,000".
- Gentle default goal of 5,000 steps, changeable in Settings in steps of 500.
- "Let's have it!" when the step goal is hit.
- Her steps show on Walking Selfie Stick's "Her day" tab; she can switch "Share my steps" off on her Sharing page. His own steps show on "My day".

Tap Update in the app, or install `SipSnack-0.4.0.apk` below. It keeps her logs.
